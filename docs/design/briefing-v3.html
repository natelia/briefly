●  ONE NEW THING — outside your usual path
CRDTs: how Figma and Google Docs merge edits with no one saying "wait your turn"
3 min · a concept from distributed systems
Two people edit the same document. The obvious answer is a lock — one edits, the other waits. Painful. The database answer is a transaction — everyone waits a little. Also painful.

A CRDT (conflict-free replicated data type) takes a third path: every edit carries a tiny bit of math so that no matter what order the edits arrive in, everyone converges to the same result. No lock, no referee, no central "yes".

That's why your Google Doc works offline and merges cleanly when you reconnect — and why sync engines are quietly becoming a big deal in app architecture.
